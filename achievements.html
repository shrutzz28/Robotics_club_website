Awards & Grants

Competitions Won

Our innovative initiatives have consistently earned recognition and financial backing from prestigious government and academic institutions. These accolades serve as a powerful testament to the viability of our work, validating our ability to bridge the gap between academic research and practical, real-world applications. By securing both national funding and institutional honors, we have demonstrated a proven track record of technical excellence and entrepreneurial potential:

  • AICTE Innovation Grant (2023): Awarded ₹2,00,000 in recognition of our technical merit and the scalable impact of our research-driven solutions.
  • Startup Seed Grant (2022): Received foundational support from the Government of India, providing the critical momentum needed to transition our concepts into reality.
  • Institute Innovation Award: Honored by NIT Patna for outstanding creativity and excellence within the academic and startup ecosystem.

Research Publications

Our research culture is built on the pillars of innovation and real-world impact, driving us to solve complex engineering challenges with academic rigor. This dedication has resulted in a strong portfolio of peer-reviewed publications within highly regarded international conferences and journals, showcasing our contributions to the global scientific community. By consistently presenting our findings at prestigious forums, we ensure our technological advancements are validated by industry experts and academic peers:

  • Autonomous Navigation using AI (IEEE ICIC 2023): Featured at the International Conference on Intelligent Computing, this work explores cutting-edge artificial intelligence frameworks for independent robotic movement.
  • IoT-Based Smart Surveillance (Springer LNNS 2022): Published in the Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems, this research focuses on integrated sensor networks and real-time security monitoring.
  • Energy-Efficient Robotics (NCAI 2021): Presented at the National Conference on Artificial Intelligence, highlighting our early breakthroughs in optimizing power consumption for automated systems.
