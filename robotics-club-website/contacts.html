Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about joining and participating in the Robotics Club NIT Patna.

How do I join the Robotics Club NIT Patna?

You can join by attending any of our weekly meetings, filling out the contact form above with the purpose "Join Club", or contacting our coordinators. There are no membership fees, and all NIT Patna students are welcome regardless of branch or experience level.

Do I need prior robotics or programming experience to join?

No prior experience is required! We welcome members of all skill levels. We offer beginner workshops on Arduino, ROS, and Python, intermediate projects, and advanced research in robotics and AI to accommodate everyone.

What kind of events and projects does the club host?

We host robotics workshops, hackathons, guest lectures by industry experts, national competitions participation, research projects in autonomous systems, drones, robotic arms, and AI applications. We also participate in national-level robotics competitions.

Are there any membership fees or requirements?

There are no membership fees. The only requirement is that you must be a student of NIT Patna. We encourage regular participation and enthusiasm for learning robotics and automation technologies.

What resources and equipment are available to club members?

We have a dedicated lab with Arduino kits, Raspberry Pi, 3D printers, sensors, motors, drones, robotic arms, and other essential equipment. Members also get access to online resources, tutorials, and mentorship from senior members and faculty advisors.
