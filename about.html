About Our Club

A community of innovators, engineers, and dreamers redefining the landscape of automation and artificial intelligence.

Our History

The NIT Patna Robotics Club, founded around 2015-16 by Nripendra Saroj Sir, fosters innovation in automation, electronics, and coding, offering students hands-on experience in designing, programming, and building robots for competitions like Smart India Hackathon. This section highlights its history, mission to enhance technical skills, key leadership, and collaborative environment for aspiring engineers, bridging theory with practical application.

We focus on fostering a culture of "Learning by Doing," where students from all branches collaborate on complex robotic systems and AI models.

Core Focus Areas

  • 🚀 Competitions: Representing NITP at Robocon, e-Yantra, and International Rover Challenges.
  • 🛠️ Workshops: Regular bootcamps on Arduino, ROS, PCB Design, and Computer Vision.
  • 🔬 Research: Interdisciplinary projects in swarm robotics and autonomous navigation.
🎯

Mission

To provide an environment where students can explore the intricacies of robotics through hands-on experience and collaborative learning.

🔭

Vision

To become a center of excellence in robotics research that contributes to global technological advancements and ethical AI development.

✅

Objectives

Foster innovation, develop industry-ready skills, and maintain a repository of open-source technical knowledge for the student community.

Our Lab & Infrastructure

The Innovation Cell

Equipped with high-end workstations, 3D printers, and various sensors for prototyping autonomous vehicles and drones.

Electronics Suite

Dedicated space for PCB fabrication, soldering, and embedded systems testing.

Club Milestones

2024

Secured Top 5 Rank in the National Rover Challenge and published 3 research papers.

2022

Establishment of the Autonomous Systems Division and high-end AI lab upgrades.

2018

Hosted the first Regional Robotics Symposium with over 10 invited colleges.

2015

The Robotics Club was officially established at NIT Patna by a group of visionary tech enthusiasts.
